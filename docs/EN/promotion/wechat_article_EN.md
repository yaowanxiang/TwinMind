# I Built an AI Twin That Learns Your Principles of Action — Then Teaches You with the Wisdom of All Humanity

> Know yourself. Learn from all of humanity. Act with wisdom.
> — The story behind TwinMind, an open-source project

Have you ever asked yourself: **how well do you actually know yourself?**

Not your zodiac sign, not your MBTI — but: *when you hit a problem, how do you actually decide? Could you explain the "playbook" you use to solve problems?*

Most people can't. We never review our own behavior — like a fish that doesn't notice it's in water.

Then one day I started thinking: **What if AI didn't just "answer my questions" — but quietly learned *how I solve problems*, then told me "you have a blind spot here, try a different angle"?**

That's how **TwinMind (Digital Portrait · Wisdom Engine)** was born — an open-source AI engine built for **every person, in every industry**.

---

## 1. The Problem It Solves

In the AI era, everyone has an "external brain." But the external brain has a fatal flaw: **it doesn't know you.**

- You spend 10 minutes explaining your background to AI every session — because it can't remember who you are;
- It gives you "standard answers" — not *your* answers;
- You step into the same trap repeatedly — AI never warns you, because it never reviews you.

TwinMind's approach is completely different: **understand you first, then help you.**

It quietly records everything you say and do (AI conversations, journals, even screen recordings), distills your **principles of action** — then uses the **wisdom of all humanity** (across cultures, eras, and disciplines) to help you solve problems from a higher dimension.

One sentence: **The more you use it, the better it knows you — better than you know yourself.**

## 2. Three Signature Capabilities

### Capability 1: 3-Level Abstraction — From "What You Did" to "Who You Are"

TwinMind is not satisfied with remembering what you did. It distills every experience into three layers:

```
L1 Concrete action    Today I cleaned my dataset with Python
L2 Thinking pattern   Build the skeleton first, fill in details, verify step by step
L3 Principle          Pursue thorough completion — never leave loose ends
```

**L3 is your true self.** These principles hold in *any* industry, *any* scenario — this is your digital portrait.

You'll be surprised to discover: the underlying logic you use for work, investing, and relationships is actually the same set.

### Capability 2: Space-Time Matrix × Cross-Discipline — The Wisdom of All Humanity at Your Service

This is TwinMind's most luxurious feature — a built-in **Human Wisdom Library**:

- **Horizontal**: How does China solve it? America? Japan?
- **Vertical**: How did the ancients solve it? Modern methods? How will the future solve it?
- **Discipline**: Military, medicine, finance, computer science, statistics…

Ask "how do I promote my open-source project?" → it teaches you about *Borrowing Arrows from Straw Boats* (leverage) and *Vertical & Horizontal Alliances* (coalition). Ask "my team is inefficient" → it brings you *Lean Thinking* (eliminate waste) and *Less is More*. Ask "should I quit my job to start a business?" → it brings you *Evolution* (small experiments) and *Controlled Variables* (change one thing at a time).

**Professional barriers are broken here.**

### Capability 3: First-Principles Reframing + Four-Poles One-Strike — Discard Your Old Habits, Rebuild from the Essence

This is the boldest move. TwinMind first asks: **What result do you truly want?**

Then it dares to do one thing: **discard all your past operations, and rebuild a plan from the essence of the problem.**

Your methods are often just *habits*, not *optimums*. The plan AI derives from first principles is often shorter, cheaper, more effective — that's the dimensional strike.

## 3. Security: Everything Done in Your Name Requires Your Approval

"AI is so smart — what if it misbehaves?"

TwinMind builds security into its architecture:

- **Risk tiering**: local-read / local-write / external communication / high-impact (transfers, deletions)
- **3 authorization modes**: Full-auto (read-only runs automatically) / Semi-auto (important actions need your nod) / Manual (every action needs you)
- **Malicious-prompt guard**: dangerous commands, prompt injection, sensitive intents auto-blocked
- **Full audit trail**: every action traceable, secrets auto-redacted

**It will never email or message anyone behind your back.** External actions require your approval.

## 4. Get Started in 30 Seconds

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt

twinmind record --hermes     # import your AI assistant's sessions
twinmind record --journal "What I did today, how I did it…"   # or write a journal
twinmind distill            # distill your principles
twinmind ask "I've been under a lot of pressure lately"   # ask, answered with humanity's wisdom

twinmind server --desktop   # open the visual desktop
```

Don't know the command line? No problem — the desktop app has five Chinese (and now English) tabs. **My 8-year-old nephew can use it.**
